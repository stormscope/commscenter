At home, calling abroad Calls to family in Sweden (or any allowed country) go out through Twilio instead of your carrier's international rate.

How it works

Callerdials your mobile (travel mode on) or the CommsCentral number
→
Twilio number+41, about 1.15 USD/month, never leaves Switzerland
→
Twilio Functions/voice/inbound reads settings from Sync, rings the app for 25 s
→
Groundwire appregistered to your SIP Domain over TLS + SRTP
→
No answer?Voicemail is recorded and emailed as an MP3 (SMS fallback)

Outbound is the same path in reverse: the app sends the call to /sip/outbound, which normalizes the number (079…, 0046…, +46…), checks it against your allowed countries and dials out with your caller ID. Everything is deployed by Pulumi from the repo. Runtime settings live in a Twilio Sync document that the admin UI edits, so redeploys don't overwrite them.

What it does

  • Calls in and out over the app, worldwide, at Swiss rates
  • Your own voicemail, emailed with the recording attached
  • Optional ringing of your SIM too, with "press 1 to accept" so carrier voicemail can't pick up
  • Blocks every country you didn't allow, plus Swiss premium numbers
  • Email/SMS alerts when monthly spend passes 20 and 50 USD

What it doesn't do

  • Emergency or short numbers (112, 1818…): use the mobile network
  • Ring you without data while travel mode is on: calls go to voicemail
  • SMS on your number (Swiss local numbers may not support it)
  • Multiple users, ring groups, IVR menus: planned, not built
  • Take over your mobile number: it stays with your carrier

Watch out for

  • Only use the unconditional forward abroad. "No answer" forwarding (**61*) still routes the call to you abroad and back, which is the cost this avoids.
  • Turn travel mode off when you're back, or your SIM never rings.
  • The Swiss number needs Twilio's regulatory approval (address and ID) once, which takes 1 to 3 business days. Bootstrap waits for it.
  • Back up ~/.commscentral/passphrase. Without it, the encrypted Pulumi config can't be read.
  • The first run is the real test. All code is unit-tested, but it hasn't run against a live Twilio account yet. Expect a fix or two.
  • Your carrier bills the forward leg, usually as a national call that's included in unlimited Swiss plans. Worth checking once.
  • Roaming data costs about 0.5–1 MB per minute of call. Prefer Wi-Fi or a local eSIM.
  • Resend without your own domain only sends to your Resend account's email address.
